# Introducing the Blazorise Indie License

Today, we're introducing **Blazorise Indie**, a new license designed to make Blazorise more accessible to independent developers and small product creators. Available for **€149 per year per registered project**, Indie offers an affordable entry point for developers who want to build and sell their own Blazor applications. {.lead}

## Why we're introducing Indie

For years, our Community license has provided individual developers with a free way to use Blazorise. While the intention behind it was good, managing the program has become increasingly challenging as Blazorise has grown.

Reviewing Community license applications has become a significant part of our daily work. Many applications were for internal company projects, which the Community license was never intended to cover. This has made the application and approval process increasingly difficult to manage as our user base continues to grow.

We want to spend more of our time improving Blazorise, developing new components, fixing bugs, and delivering a better experience for everyone.

At the same time, we recognize that the cost of a Professional license can be a barrier for independent developers, especially those who are just starting out or whose products are not yet generating significant revenue.

Indie is our way of addressing both challenges. It simplifies our licensing process, gives independent developers a more affordable entry point, and helps us build a sustainable business that can continue to grow and invest in Blazorise.

## Who is Indie for?

Blazorise Indie is designed for solo developers who build and own their own products.

Each license covers one registered project with an annual revenue below **€25,000**, whether it's a personal project, a side business, a SaaS application, or another independently developed product.

With an Indie license, you can:

- Build and sell your own Blazor applications.
- Use Blazorise without evaluation component limits.
- Remove the licensing banner from your registered project.
- Start building commercially for just €149 per year.

Indie is intended for independently owned and developed products. It does not cover client projects, freelance contracts, agency work, or internal company applications.

## What about support?

Indie is a self-service license designed to keep the cost as low as possible.

Indie users have access to our regular community-based support through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Megabit/Blazorise/issues), where they can report bugs, suggest improvements, and participate in discussions alongside the wider Blazorise community.

Dedicated technical support is not included with Indie and remains a benefit of our Professional and higher licensing plans.

## A license that grows with your product

We don't want licensing costs to stand in the way of your next idea.

Indie allows you to start small, launch your product, find your first customers, and grow your business without committing to a more expensive license from the beginning.

Once your project's annual revenue reaches €25,000, you'll need to upgrade to Professional at your next renewal. You can also upgrade at any time if you need dedicated support or additional benefits.

You can compare all available plans on our [pricing page](https://blazorise.com/pricing).

## **What happens to the Community license?**

With the introduction of Indie, we are officially retiring the Community license. New applications and renewals will no longer be accepted.

If you already hold an approved Community license, you can continue using it under its original terms **until its current expiration date**.

Once your Community license expires, it cannot be renewed or extended. To continue using Blazorise without evaluation limitations, you will need to transition to Indie or another licensing plan that suits your needs.

## Building a sustainable future for Blazorise

Blazorise has grown significantly over the years, both as a component library and as a business. We want to continue that growth while making our tools accessible to developers at different stages of their journey.

We believe Indie strikes a better balance between affordability for independent developers and the long-term sustainability of Blazorise.

It allows us to spend less time managing licenses and more time building the tools you use every day. And for independent developers, it provides a clear, affordable path from an idea to a commercial product.

Learn more and get started on our [Blazorise Indie page](https://blazorise.com/indie).
